# Reflection

Working on this project helped our team learn more about GPT-5 and its real-world applications. AI tools made it easier to research, summarize information, and organize our presentation into clear sections.

We also used AI to evaluate our work, which provided useful feedback on our presentation's strengths and areas for improvement. Comparing AI feedback showed that different AI models can provide different suggestions, helping us improve the quality of our work.

Although AI was helpful throughout the project, our team reviewed the information carefully to make sure it was accurate and suitable for the presentation. This project showed us that AI is a valuable tool for learning and software development, but human judgment is still important.
