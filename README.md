## Project Summary

This project explores **GPT-5**, an advanced AI model developed by OpenAI as the successor to GPT-4 and GPT-4o. GPT-5 is part of OpenAI's Generative Pre-trained Transformer (GPT) family and is designed to understand and generate human-like text, solve problems, write code, and analyse information across multiple formats. Our presentation covers what GPT-5 is, its new features, its real-world applications, and the challenges and future implications of this technology.

---

## Key Findings

### What is GPT-5?
- An advanced AI model developed by OpenAI, built to understand and generate human-like text, solve problems, write code, and analyse information.
- Represents a major step forward in AI's ability to reason through and handle complex, multi-step tasks.

### New Features
- **Improved Reasoning** — better at solving complex, multi-step problems, adapting effort to the difficulty of the task.
- **Better Coding** — more capable of writing, debugging, and understanding larger and more complex code.
- **More Accurate Responses** — improved instruction-following with fewer factual mistakes than earlier models.
- **Multimodal Capabilities** — can work with both text and images to help analyse information in different formats.
- **Better Performance** — faster and more efficient, adapting its reasoning depth to the complexity of the task.

### Real-World Applications
- **Education** — personalised tutoring, help with difficult concepts, and support for teachers with lesson planning.
- **Healthcare** — summarising medical information and helping patients understand health information in simpler language.
- **Business** — automating repetitive tasks, analysing data, generating reports, and supporting decision-making.
- **Software Development** — writing, explaining, and debugging code, and speeding up documentation and testing.

### Challenges and Future Outlook
- AI can still produce incorrect, misleading, or outdated information — human review remains important for major decisions.
- Privacy is a growing concern as AI systems process large amounts of personal and confidential data.
- AI may automate some jobs while also creating new AI-related roles and skill demands.
- Future development will likely focus on safety, reliability, privacy, and responsible use, with humans continuing to play a key supervisory role.
